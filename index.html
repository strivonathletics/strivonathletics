Parent FAQ

Questions families usually ask.

+

Who are Strivon mentors?

Current college student-athletes who recently went through recruiting themselves. They share firsthand experience, not professional admissions advice.

+

Can parents join the call?

Yes — parents are welcome on every call. You can choose who's attending when you book.

+

Is Strivon an admissions consulting service?

No. Mentors share their own recruiting experience and guidance — they are not admissions officers and don't make or influence admissions decisions.

+

Can Strivon guarantee that my athlete will be recruited?

No. We don't guarantee admission, coach interest, roster spots, or pre-read results — we provide guidance based on personal experience and general information.

+

How are mentors matched with athletes?

Based on a short intake — sport, academics, school interests, and what you want help with — scored against each mentor's own background. You can also browse the full roster and choose yourself.

+

What can we discuss during a call?

School fit, coach outreach, camps, pre-reads, recruiting timeline, and whatever questions are on your mind — the goal is to leave with clear next steps.

+

What happens if none of the available mentors are a good match?

Reach out to us directly and we'll help point you to the closest fit, or let you know honestly if we don't have a good match yet.
